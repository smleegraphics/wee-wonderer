  - Create baseline experiences, not the pipeline

## Themes
- **San Francisco**
  - Cute little dioramas of different san francisco neighborhoods
  - Live infinite generation vs true to map data
- **Underground tunnels college campus**
- **Mini figurine exploring world (smiski?)**
  - Run around your own house as a smiski
- **Photo coming to life via 4d gaussian splat (VFX)**
  - Gallery of 10 locations — maybe figurine can walk through polaroid
    into gsplat?
- **Music based experience building** — starting web page is record player
  table where you pick albums to play and it opens pre generated experiences
  (could be live proc gen, splat, etc) that the creator made inspired by
  the music

## Smiski Idea (the one we picked)
1. Take a scan of an environment
2. Open a web browser app and load your scan in
3. Press play and a smiski character spawns that you can move around the
   scene, climb on different surfaces
